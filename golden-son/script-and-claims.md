# GOLDEN SON — explained in under 2 minutes (DRAFT, awaiting approval)

Status: script pass of the fact-check is done (below). No voiceover generated yet.

## Script (~215 words, about 1:35 of narration)

House Bellona wants Darrow dead. So he jumps onto their dinner table... and smiles. How did he get here?

Years after the Institute, Darrow serves ArchGovernor Augustus — the man who hanged his wife. Then he loses. At the Academy, Karnus au Bellona beats him. Augustus decides to sell his contract. Darrow is finished.

The rebels have one last plan: carry a bomb into the Sovereign's gala. He doesn't. Instead, he climbs onto the Bellona table and challenges Cassius to a duel. In front of everyone. Darrow wins. He takes Cassius's arm. The Sovereign steps in. And the gala becomes a bloodbath.

Just like that... civil war. Augustus against Bellona. Darrow needs to take Mars. So he drops an army from the sky. The Iron Rain. Mars falls.

Along the way, he learns who leads the rebellion. Ares... is Fitchner. His old Proctor from the Institute.

And he shows Mustang the truth. He was born a Red. She leaves without giving him an answer.

Darrow takes Mars. They throw him a Triumph. His friend Roque hands him a box. Inside... is Fitchner's head. Roque drugs him. The Jackal kills his own father. And Darrow is taken prisoner.

He won the war. And lost everything at his own victory party.
