## 1. Final narration (260 words, about 1:50 at channel pace)

House Bellona wants Darrow dead. So he walks across their dinner table... and asks for a fight. How did he get here?

Years after the Institute, Darrow serves ArchGovernor Augustus. The man who hanged his wife. Then he loses. At the Academy, Karnus au Bellona beats him. Augustus moves to sell his contract. Darrow is finished.

The rebels offer one last move: carry a bomb into the Sovereign's gala. He carries it in... and never sets it off. Instead, he climbs onto the Bellona table and challenges Cassius to a duel. In front of everyone. Darrow wins. He takes Cassius's sword arm. The Sovereign stops the duel. The Bellona attack. And the gala becomes a bloodbath.

Just like that... civil war. Augustus against Bellona.

Darrow builds an army. Ragnar, an Obsidian warrior he sets free. Lorn, the old Rage Knight who taught him the blade. Sevro and his Howlers. Then he drops that army from orbit, straight onto Mars. The Iron Rain. Darrow takes the planet.

And he learns who leads the rebellion. Ares... is Fitchner. His old Proctor from the Institute. Sevro's father.

He takes Mustang home. To the mines. And shows her the truth: he was born a Red. She leaves without giving him an answer.

They throw him a Triumph. At the feast, his friend Roque drugs him. Then comes a box. Inside... is Fitchner's head. The Jackal kills his own father. And Darrow is taken prisoner.

He walked across a table to start a war. He won it. And lost everything at his own victory feast.

**End card:** punchline "HE WON THE WAR. / **HE LOST EVERYTHING.**" then question (Cinzel small caps) "CAN ROQUE EVER BE FORGIVEN?" + gold answer line "YES OR NO — MORNING STAR NEXT?"

## 4. Changes from the previous draft, and why

1. **Hook rewritten:** "jumps onto their dinner table... and smiles" became "walks across their dinner table... and asks for a fight." The table is confirmed by four sources; the smile is not sourced anywhere, so it stays as a visual acting choice only and is no longer narrated.
2. **Last line now reframes the hook:** "He walked across a table to start a war. He won it. And lost everything at his own victory feast." (v1 ending did not call back to the table.)
3. **Bomb beat fixed:** v1 said "carry a bomb into the gala. He doesn't." — that reads as if he never brought it. Sources say he brings it, plants it, then takes it back. Now: "He carries it in... and never sets it off."
4. **"Sword arm"** instead of "arm" in narration — that is the exact wording of three sources; left/right is unverified, so nothing in the script names a side.
5. **"The Sovereign steps in" became "The Sovereign stops the duel. The Bellona attack."** — more precise and matches SuperSummary/Wikipedia; makes it clear who starts the bloodbath.
6. **Removed the duplicate "Darrow takes Mars"** (v1 said it twice) and the redundant "Darrow needs to take Mars."
7. **Added one line each for Ragnar, Lorn and Sevro** (all verified) as an "army" beat, so the Iron Rain has faces. Sevro also pays off as "Sevro's father" on the Fitchner reveal.
8. **"Along the way, he learns..." became "And he learns..."** placed after the Iron Rain: sources disagree only on whether it is during or just after the Mars fighting, and both put it at/after the rain, not before.
9. **Mustang beat now says where:** "He takes Mustang home. To the mines." (Lykos, confirmed by SuperSummary + two others.)
10. **Triumph order corrected:** v1 had Roque hand over the box and then drug him. Sources have the drugging first/at the dinner and disagree on who opens the box. Now: "Roque drugs him. Then comes a box."
11. **"Victory party" became "victory feast"** to match the sources (a feast/dinner in his honor).
12. **Length:** 215 → 260 words to hit the 230-270 target.
13. **Captions added** for every beat, plus an end-card question.

## 5. Visuals team: do not get these wrong

- **Table (shots 1, 8):** it is the **House Bellona** table (eagle sigil), not the Augustus table (lion). Darrow is **standing on it**; **Cassius is seated at it, below him**. The bomb was under the *Augustus* table — do not draw it under the Bellona one.
- **Boots/plates:** one summary supports crystal crushed under his boots; keep it to broken glass/dishes. One summary also has him pour wine in Cassius's lap (single source) — optional, do not caption it.
- **Smile:** user-requested, unsourced. Fine as acting; never put it in a caption.
- **Cassius is alive after the duel.** No X over him, no "killed" wording. He loses an **arm**, his **sword arm**. Left vs right is **unverified**: stage him so the arm that held his razor in the duel shot is the one missing in the next shot (consistency inside our own video is the only rule we can enforce). Do not label it "right" or "left".
- **Who stops the duel:** the **Sovereign, Octavia au Lune** (older Gold woman, on a dais). Do not draw Aja saving Cassius. Mustang may stand between Darrow and Cassius (two sources), but do not caption it.
- **Who attacks first in the brawl:** the **Bellonas** (Wikipedia; wiki says Cassius's mother Julia urges it).
- **Karnus (shot 4):** he *beats* Darrow at the Academy (a fleet war game). Nobody dies in that shot. Sources conflict on who later kills Karnus — never show his death.
- **Ragnar:** an **Obsidian** (huge, pale, white-haired), not a Gold. Darrow **frees** him and gives him a **razor**. He is not captured/enslaved by Darrow in frame.
- **Lorn:** an **old** Gold, a retired **Rage Knight**; he lives on **Europa** (ice/ocean moon). He trained Darrow *before* the gala.
- **Iron Rain:** soldiers falling from **orbit in armored suits (starShells)** onto **Mars**; the target city is the capital, **Agea**. Not bombs, not ships landing.
- **Fitchner / Ares:** Fitchner is the same character as the **House Mars Proctor** from our Book 1 video — reuse that design. He is **Sevro's father**. Ares is shown with a helmet; Fitchner is the one under it. Do not put the Ares helmet on Dancer or Harmony.
- **Mustang reveal:** setting is **Lykos**, the Red mining colony, in the corridor **outside his family's home**. Mustang watches a **holoCube recording of the carving alone**; Darrow is **inside**, not next to her. She **leaves**; no answer, no gun (the gunpoint detail is single-source), no tears-and-hug, no betrayal.
- **Triumph:** **Roque** (Darrow's friend, the poet; Gold) is the one who **drugs** Darrow — not the Jackal, not Cassius. Darrow is **paralyzed**, not unconscious, when the box is shown. The **box contains Fitchner's head** (keep it non-graphic). Do not show a specific hand opening the box if avoidable (sources differ).
- **The Jackal kills Nero au Augustus** (his own father, the ArchGovernor = the man who hanged Eo). The Jackal has **one hand** (Book 1). Do not show Darrow, Roque or Cassius killing Nero. Fitchner's killer is not shown at all.
- **Darrow is captured, alive.** No death imagery for Darrow. Sevro, Ragnar and Mustang are **not** captured or killed in the final shots — keep them out of the Triumph room.
- **Sevro:** keep the Book 1 design (dark hair, gray wolf pelt, eye patch — side still unverified per CLAUDE.md).
- **Darrow's weapon:** in the duel he uses a **razor** (Gold blade); the modest steel sickle rule from CLAUDE.md still applies elsewhere.
- **Not verified / single-source items to avoid drawing as fact:** which arm; who opens the box; Mustang at gunpoint; wine in Cassius's lap; who kills Lorn; whether Cassius's arm is reattached or replaced.
