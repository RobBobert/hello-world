## Voiceover

To destroy the Golds... he had to become one.

Mars. Darrow is sixteen — a Helldiver, the best miner in Lykos. The Reds dig to make Mars livable for humanity. But Mars has been livable for centuries. His clan earns the prize for the most ore. The Golds give it away. His wife, Eo, wants more. She sings the forbidden song — in front of everyone. They hang her. Darrow pulls her feet to end her pain. He buries her. That's a crime. So they hang him too. He wakes up — rescued by rebels.

The Sons of Ares have a plan: carve him into a Gold. New bones. New eyes. Gold hair. He enters the Institute — where Golds learn to rule. The first night: kill a classmate. He kills Julian. Cassius's brother. Then the war game begins. Twelve houses. One valley. His own house turns savage. Darrow kills their leader, Titus — and takes command. They call him the Reaper. House after house falls.

But the judges are cheating — for the Jackal. So what are his odds? The judges have armor, weapons, and training. Darrow has surprise. He raids Olympus... and chains the judges. The Jackal tries to bargain. Darrow takes his hand. Darrow wins the Institute. His prize? To serve the ArchGovernor. The man who hanged Eo.

He became a Gold to destroy them. Now... he has to kneel to one.
