## Why it's built this way

- **Hook = paradox, not plot.** Beat 1 states the contradiction the whole story runs on. Beat 37 pays it off with the same split-face image, so the ending reframes the opening — same trick as "They sacked a fortress / just to rob their ally."
- **Escalation ladder:** slave → hanged → remade → wins → serves his wife's killer → exposed → buried alive → rises → breaks the system → stuck ruling the wreckage. Every rung is worse or bigger than the last.
- **Re-hooks every ~28s** (0:27 "one plan", 0:57 "the odds", 1:25 "dug him out"). A 2-min short loses people at the 30s/60s marks; each act opens with a question or reversal.
- **The Odds card** is reused from both references — viewers of the channel recognize it, and it sets up the "one secret" that pays off in the very next act.
